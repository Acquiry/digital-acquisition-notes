# Subscription billing migration when buying a SaaS business

*By Joash Boyton, CEO of Acquiry, Melbourne.*

When a buyer acquires a SaaS business, the subscriptions do not always move as easily as the code. Customer payment details sit with a billing provider and a payment processor, and moving them takes planning.

## Find out where billing lives

Identify the billing system, the payment processor, the merchant account holder and the entity that customers have contracted with. In small businesses, these may be held personally by the founder or by a different company from the one being sold.

## Asset sale or share sale

In a share sale, the entity stays the same and billing usually continues. In an asset sale, the buyer may need its own accounts, and customer payment data may need to be migrated. Processors have their own rules for migrating card data, and some migrations require customers to re-enter details, which causes churn.

## Plan for failed payments

Any change in billing descriptor, entity or processor can raise failed payment rates. A buyer should model a temporary rise in involuntary churn, plan customer communications in advance, and set up dunning before the switch.
